The first release of CutterSnap: take a photo of a cookie and get a sturdy, smooth,
3D-printable cookie cutter. It runs on your own machine in Docker, with classical computer
vision only: no AI models, no accounts and no cloud.

## Install

```sh
docker run -d --name cuttersnap --restart unless-stopped \
  -p 8080:8080 -v cuttersnap-designs:/data \
  ghcr.io/addison16/cuttersnap:0.1.0
```

Then open http://localhost:8080, or the computer's local address from a phone on the same
Wi-Fi. The image runs on 64-bit PCs, Macs and Raspberry Pis (`linux/amd64` and `linux/arm64`).
A Docker Compose file and update steps are in the
[README](https://github.com/Addison16/CutterSnap#install-with-docker).

## What's in it

**From photo to outline**
- Automatic tracing that finds the cookie's outer edge and ignores icing details.
- Cookie and Not cookie clicks for busy photos.
- Edge points for white-on-white cookies and striped icing: click round the edge and the
  outline follows the photo between your points.
- Edit as points, Zoom to box, and orange rings where corners were rounded.

**A cutter that prints and cuts well**
- A 2.4 x 6 mm base with a chamfer into the wall, so corners don't snap or warp.
- A blade sized to your nozzle that tapers to two printed lines. It is slice-tested in
  PrusaSlicer with both classic and Arachne walls.
- Points rounded to at least 1.5 mm and notches to at least 2.5 mm, re-checked after smoothing.
- Smooth curves: the STL's flat facets stay within about 0.02 mm of the true curve.
- A dough spread setting, so the baked cookie matches the photo.
- The cutter is built mirrored, because you turn it over to cut, so the cookie comes out the
  same way round as the photo.

**Extras**
- A pusher plate to push dough out of narrow parts.
- A matching stamp made from the icing lines in the photo, with adjustable detail, line
  width and depth.
- Initials (up to 3 letters) pressed into the underside of the base.
- A halo border, and a mirror image for left and right pairs.
- Size presets for 5, 7.5 and 10 cm, with the finished cookie size shown on screen.

**Keeping designs**
- My designs keeps a design on your CutterSnap, so one made on a phone is ready to download
  on the computer by your printer.
- Project files rebuild the same STL byte for byte.
- A command line for scripting: `cuttersnap photo.jpg cutter.stl`.
- Works on a phone: big touch targets and a one-column layout.

## Known limits

- My designs has no sign-in. Anyone who can open the page can see and delete designs, so keep
  CutterSnap on your home network.
- The blade is slice-tested in PrusaSlicer only. OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio and Cura are
  untested, though OrcaSlicer uses the same Arachne wall generator by default.
- Printed cutters are not food-safe in any certified sense. Wash by hand in warm water and
  replace them when the edge gets rough.
