    Printing and care tips
    • Print base-down with no supports and no brim. The cutting edge is two nozzle widths, so your slicer lays it down as two full lines.
    • Put the seam on the outside or "random", not on a sharp point of the blade.
    • PETG lasts longer than PLA. PLA softens around 50 °C, so wash cutters by hand in warm, not hot, water and keep them out of the dishwasher and hot cars.
    • Layer lines hold on to dough and germs. Wash and dry right after use and treat printed cutters as seasonal; replace them when the edge gets rough.
    • Dusting the blade with flour helps dough release. The pusher plate pushes dough out of narrow parts without tearing it.
